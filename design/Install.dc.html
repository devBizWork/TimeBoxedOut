Add Routine Tracker to your Home Screen

iPhone doesn't offer to install web apps, so here's how. It takes about ten seconds.

Opens full screen Works offline Safari is less likely to clear your data
  1. 1 Tap ShareIn Safari's toolbar, below this page
  2. 2 Choose Add to Home ScreenScroll down the menu if you don't see it
  3. 3 Tap AddThen open Routine Tracker from your Home Screen

On Android, open the Chrome menu and tap Install app.
